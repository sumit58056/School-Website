From the Principal's Desk

"Education is the manifestation of the perfection already in man."

At Vivekananda Mission, we believe in nurturing not just the intellect, but the character of our students. In strict alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, our curriculum seamlessly blends modern scientific inquiry with the profound wisdom of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS).

Our infrastructure and pedagogical approaches are evolving to meet the visionary standards set by the PM SHRI schools initiative. We strive to create holistic, well-rounded citizens who are globally competent yet deeply connected to their cultural roots and ethical values.
