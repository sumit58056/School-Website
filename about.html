About VMI

Discover our history, mission, and educational philosophy

Our Motto

"Educate · Enlighten · Innovate"

Our History

Established with a commitment to excellence, Vivekananda Mission Institute has grown into a premier educational institution in Muluk, Bolpur. Rooted in the timeless ideals of Swami Vivekananda, we strive to build character, cultivate intellect, and inspire a lifelong love for learning in every student who walks through our doors.

NEP 2020 & IKS Integration

Aligned with Govt. of India Standards

We proudly implement the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 frameworks alongside the rich traditions of the Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS). Our multidisciplinary approach ensures that students develop 21st-century analytical skills while remaining deeply grounded in India's cultural, scientific, and intellectual heritage.

👁️

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence that nurtures holistic development, creating responsible global citizens who are intellectually sharp, emotionally balanced, and deeply rooted in strong moral values.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide a dynamic, inclusive, and innovative learning environment. We empower students to discover their unique potential, foster critical thinking, and contribute meaningfully to society.
